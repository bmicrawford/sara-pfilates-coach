Three weeks in, keep going. Bowel control often takes a little longer to change than bladder control.

- Regular meals, fluids and fiber as your clinician advises can support regular, comfortable bowel movements.
- See your doctor for any new change in bowel habits, bleeding, or accidents that are getting worse.
- Keep your 5 + 5 going and your pulse sharp.
