Hi {{first_name | default: 'there'}}, you told us leakage after prostate surgery, or when you cough, lift or exercise, is your main concern.
- Check with your surgeon or urologist about when to start, especially if a catheter was recently removed.
- Make the pulse sharp. A short, forceful, open-mouth "HA" with a voluntary squeeze (picture stepping into cold water), at the exact moment of the pulse.
- Pre-squeeze in real life. Try a quick voluntary pelvic floor squeeze before you stand up, cough, sneeze or lift.
