Three weeks in, I want you to know how common this is.

- Only 29% of women with urinary leakage see a clinician about it, about 3 in 10 (Hou 2025). (This applies to urinary incontinence in general, comes from the study abstract, and the studies varied widely. It's about seeking care, not about treatment results.)
- If urgency is new, painful, or comes with blood in your urine, see your doctor promptly.
- When urgency hits, stop, do a few quick squeezes, and let the wave pass before you walk to the bathroom.
