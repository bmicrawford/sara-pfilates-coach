Hi {{first_name | default: 'there'}}, you told us frequent urination and/or difficulty making it to the bathroom in time is your main concern.
- Use your bladder diary as a map. Note the times, the drinks, and the moments urgency hits. Patterns help.
- When an urge hits: stop, stand or sit still, do a few quick voluntary pelvic floor squeezes, breathe, and let the urge settle before you walk calmly to the bathroom.
- Don't cut fluids drastically. Spread them through the day, and notice whether caffeine or fizzy drinks make your urgency worse.
- Keep your 5 + 5 going with your best 2–3 movements and a sharp pulse.
