- Long-term follow-up (Fenocchi 2023): over 10+ years, 43.6% of women with prolapse who did pelvic floor muscle training needed further prolapse treatment, compared with 52.8% of women who didn't train. That's a modest difference. The statistics were close to the margin, it looked at pelvic floor muscle training in general, and it did not test PfilAtes™.
