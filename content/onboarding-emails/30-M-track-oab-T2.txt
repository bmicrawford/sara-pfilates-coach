Three weeks in, keep going. Steady daily practice matters more than any single session.

- When urgency hits, stop, do a few quick squeezes, and let the wave pass before you walk to the bathroom.
- Repeat your bladder diary on Days 25–28 and compare.
- If urgency is new, painful, comes with blood in your urine, or comes with a weak stream, see your doctor.
