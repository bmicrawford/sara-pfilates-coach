Six weeks in, your body isn't where it was on day one. Movements that scored a 1 back then might score a 3 now. That makes this a good time to re-take your Movement Selection Worksheet.

How to do it (about 15 minutes):
1. Print a fresh worksheet from Outline and Objectives → Downloads.
2. Replay the Movement Selection Video (Learn the Movements). You can skip around to the movements you want to test.
3. Score each movement again: pain? could you do it every day? sensation 0–3?
4. Pick your new best 2–3 and carry on with 5 minutes in the morning and 5 in the evening.

Remember to pulse at every hold point: small range + "HA" + squeeze.
