1. Your movements. Are your 2–3 movements really your best? Pull out your worksheet. Pick the pain-free ones you could do every day that scored highest. If you're unsure, try Lunge, Bridge, Hovering, Cat Cow or Butterfly.
2. Your pulse. All three parts at the same moment: a small range at the hold point, a short, forceful, open-mouth "HA," and a voluntary squeeze (picture stepping into cold water). Re-watch the Squat in the Movement Selection Video.
3. Your consistency. Look at your exercise log. Are you getting both 5-minute sessions in most days? Tie them to things you already do every day.
4. Your measuring. Your exercise log has you repeat the bladder diary on Days 25–28. That's where you'll see your own numbers.
