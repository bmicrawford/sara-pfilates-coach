This is my fourth reminder about the pulse, because it really is the part that matters most. At your next session, ask yourself these 4 questions:

1. Did I do the full movement first, then settle at my hold point?
2. Is my pulse small? It should stay near the hold point, not go through a full repetition.
3. Is my breath percussive? A short, forceful, open-mouth exhale ("HA") that I can feel firm up my deep abdominal wall, the transversus abdominis.
4. Am I squeezing on purpose? A voluntary pelvic floor contraction, timed with the breath and the pulse.

Four yeses means you're doing PfilAtes™ the way it was designed.
