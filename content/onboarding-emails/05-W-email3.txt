You're 5 days in. Here are the three pulse mistakes I see most often, and how to fix each one.

1. The movement is too big. The pulse is a small range of motion around your hold point, which is where your pelvic floor is already working its hardest on its own. Stay close to it.

2. The breath is too soft. The percussive breath is a short, forceful, open-mouth exhale: "HA!" A slow sigh won't engage the transversus abdominis.

3. The squeeze is missing. Add a voluntary pelvic floor contraction on every pulse. The movement and the breath need the squeeze to go with them.

The key is timing: all three happen at the same instant.

Habit tip: Tie your 5 minutes to something you already do twice a day, like brushing your teeth. Morning teeth, then PfilAtes™. Evening teeth, then PfilAtes™. Keep your exercise log next to the sink.

- Can I do all 10 movements? Your best 2–3 work better for a 5-minute session. Quality beats quantity.
