Two months! Most habits get easier around now, and also easier to let slip. Three ways to stay on track:

- Keep your anchor. Brush your teeth, then do PfilAtes™, morning and evening.
- Keep it short. 5 minutes is enough, so it doesn't need to be a big event.
- Keep your pulse sharp. Small range at the hold point, a percussive open-mouth "HA" that engages the transversus abdominis, and a voluntary squeeze.
