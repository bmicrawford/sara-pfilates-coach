Now, the most important part of every movement: the pulse. The power is in the pulse.

Every PfilAtes™ movement has a hold point. That's the position where your pelvic floor reaches its peak passive activation. When you reach it, you add the pulse, which has three parts that happen at the same moment:

1. A small range of motion around the hold point. Make small, controlled pulses right at the hold point. Don't go back through the full movement each time.
2. A percussive breath. Make a short, forceful exhale with your mouth open, like Heather's "HA!" in the video. This engages your transversus abdominis, the deep corset muscle around your middle, at the exact moment of the pulse.
3. A voluntary pelvic floor contraction. Squeeze and lift your pelvic floor on purpose, as you practiced on the towel.

Small pulse + "HA" + squeeze, all together. Do the full movement first, then hold and pulse. To see it again, open Learn the Movements → Movement Selection Video and watch the Squat.
